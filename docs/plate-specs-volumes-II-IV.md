# Plate Specs — Volumes II–IV

**Purpose:** Volume I's plates were generated from per-chapter "Visual assets recommended" lists in the Guide Drafts. Volumes II–IV (21 chapters) never got that step and are text-only. This document is the generation blueprint for their plate batch — one list per chapter, in the same style, prompt-ready.

**Status:** Spec only. Nothing here is generated yet. Cut freely; nothing is canon because it is listed.

---

## House style (applies to every plate)

The ATX Settlemint treatment. Palette: Bone `#F1F0E9` ground, Ink `#111111`, Texas Blue `#006FCC`, Mission Coral `#FF8574`, Acid Sun `#F4FF00`. Labels in JetBrains Mono, headlines in Space Grotesk weight. Visible halftone/riso grain. Engraved / screen-printed feel, never glossy 3D or corporate-infographic.

Five established plate families — every new plate should be one of these:

1. **Bone info-plate** (landscape ~1448×1086) — diagram, flow, table, or map on Bone with mono labels, coral/blue accents, plate number corner. *Reference: entity-comparison, realm-relationship-model, settlemint-ecosystem.*
2. **Blueprint** (Texas Blue ground, white linework, landscape or square) — technical drawings of sites, modules, layouts. *Reference: atx-001-blueprint, settlemint-core-layout, field-module-s001.*
3. **Riso poster** (portrait ~1122×1402) — cobalt/coral engraved scene, acid sun, ATX footer bar. *Reference: the four part posters, settlement-is-the-protocol.*
4. **Scroll plate** (tall portrait) — vertical numbered sequence. *Reference: operating-stack.*
5. **Seal / cartouche** (square ~1254×1254, ink on transparent or Bone) — civic marks, badges, stamps. *Reference: the five seals shipped 2026-07-31.*

**Volume registers:** Handbook plates = warm and practical (info-plates, blueprints, loops). Standard plates = austere instrument cards — mono-heavy, checklist and gauge motifs, almost no scene illustration. Architecture plates = system diagrams and blueprints; the most restrained of the three.

## Disclosure guardrails (hard rules)

- **No FIG, Figmint, token, or currency imagery anywhere.** Economy plates stay currency-agnostic — value flows, ledgers, work.
- **Basemint gets exactly one plate, function-level only** — a door in a wall, never a mechanism, never swap/bridge/liquidity imagery.
- **Never the word or image of a "chain"** for Firmamint — substrate, not chain.
- **No internal org names** (CIK etc.) on any plate. ATX, Firma, Settlemint, District are fine.
- Everything proposed stays visually provisional — blueprints and specs, not photoreal finished product shots.

# Volume II — The Settlemint Handbook

## HB-01 — The Settlemint Stack

1. **THE MINIMUM FUNCTIONAL STACK** — bone info-plate: the eight domains (people & belonging, place & habitation, governance, economy & work, infrastructure, memory & accountability, care & continuity, external relations) as eight labeled bays in one structure.
2. **THE FUNCTIONAL FAMILIES** — bone info-plate: six horizontal strata (human / civic / economic / physical / intelligence & communications / substrate) drawn as a geological section under one settlement.
3. **THE DEPENDENCY MAP** — bone info-plate: one critical function traced through *who operates → who owns → who can disable → fallback*, with a red override line. Caption: "You do not understand your stack until you understand how it fails."
4. **INTEGRATION IS NOT CENTRALIZATION** — bone info-plate, two contrasted diagrams: a federated mesh that composes vs. a single hub that captures.
5. **THE FIVE-QUESTION MODULE TEST** — seal-stamped instrument card: the five questions a module must answer before its name becomes canon.

## HB-02 — Membership and Belonging

1. **THE TERMS THAT MUST REMAIN DISTINCT** — comparison card in the entity-comparison format: visitor / participant / member / steward / citizen-of-what, against what each is owed and owes.
2. **BELONGING AT EACH LAYER** — bone info-plate: concentric rings person → household → Settlemint → District → Nation, each ring labeled with the claim it has on you.
3. **CONTRACT AND COVENANT** — two-column plate: what a contract binds, what a covenant binds; ink document vs. seal-stamped cartouche.
4. **SUBSIDIARITY** — decision-altitude diagram: which decisions live at which layer, drawn as floors of a tower with a "lowest capable level" rule line.

## HB-03 — Maturity Stages and Proof Tests

1. **THE PROOF LADDER** — scroll plate: Claim → Signal → Evidence → Repetition → Reliability → Institution as six rungs with one-line tests.
2. **A MAP, NOT ONE SCORE** — bone info-plate: a maturity profile chart (domains on one axis, proof status on the other) with an uneven, honest silhouette.
3. **THE EVIDENCE LEDGER** — instrument card: the ledger record itself (claim / status / evidence / dependencies / failure modes / next proof) as a filled-in specimen.

## HB-04 — Governance and Dispute Resolution

1. **THE PRACTICAL QUESTIONS** — instrument card: the first governance questions (who approves spending, who commits the community, who maintains, who corrects) stamped like a site checklist.
2. **THE DISPUTE PATH** — bone info-plate flow: dispute → accepted process → resolution → record → appeal, with the jurisdictional boundary marked where internal process ends and surrounding law begins.
3. **THE FOUNDER-DEPENDENCE TEST** — two-panel plate: every line running through one figure vs. the same functions held by roles and institutions. Caption: "What stops working when the founder leaves the room?"

## HB-05 — Starting a Settlemint

1. **MORE THAN ONE ORIGIN PATH** — bone info-plate: six paths (cloud-first, place-first, covenant-first, institution-first, crisis-first, infrastructure-first) converging on the same proof threshold.
2. **THE FIRST SYSTEM IS A LOOP** — bone info-plate: one recurring weekly loop (worship, meal, work, decision, record) drawn as a circle with a flywheel arrow.
3. **BEFORE THE NAME IS CLAIMED** — gate blueprint: the minimum-viable threshold as a literal gate; the checklist posted on it.
4. **ONE FUNCTIONING SYSTEM AT A TIME** — riso poster: a settlement half-built in stages, first bay lit, rest in linework. Footer: "Unproven means proposed."

## HB-06 — Work, Economy, and Treasury

1. **WHAT AN ECONOMY MUST PROVE** — bone info-plate: production, exchange, treasury activity, public goods as four working stations; a lone coin crossed out. Caption: "A token does not prove an economy."
2. **USEFUL EXCESS AND THE COMMONS** — bone info-plate: excess at one node routed as capacity to another (energy, compute, tools, time). Caption: "What is excess to one node may be capacity to another."
3. **TREASURY DISCIPLINE** — instrument card: transparent ledger, named stewards, spending authority, review — as a treasury door with posted rules.
4. **PAYMENT, GENEROSITY, WAGE** — three-column distinctions card; what each is and is not.

## HB-07 — External Relationships

1. **RECOGNIZED BY WHOM?** — bone info-plate strip: municipal → subnational → national → diplomatic → international, each rung weighted differently on a scale motif. *(Also fills a Volume I wish from Draft 01/02.)*
2. **NAME EACH RELATIONSHIP PRECISELY** — taxonomy card: neighbor, supplier, partner, government, market, peer polity — each with what it proves and does not prove.
3. **UNDER EXISTING SOVEREIGNTY, COOPERATING DEEPLY** — riso poster: the settlement stitched into the surrounding county fabric — roads and lines continuing outward, no wall.
4. **DEPENDENCIES ARE RELATIONSHIPS** — web diagram with named counterparties and a *chosen / imposed, replaceable / captive* legend.

## HB-08 — ATX: The Implementation Case Study

1. **THE FOUR-ZONE ACCESS MODEL** — blueprint: the ATX site with its four access zones, keyed like a survey map.
2. **THE OPERATING LOOP IN PRACTICE** — bone info-plate: the actual recurring ATX rhythm as a dated weekly wheel.
3. **EVIDENCE LEDGER — SNAPSHOT** — instrument card: the July 2026 ledger snapshot as a specimen document, dated, with "not yet proved" lines left visibly open.
4. **GENESIS PROJECTS** — blueprint strip: early projects (restoration, hospitality, land systems) drawn as numbered work orders, not marketing vignettes.

# Volume III — The Firma Architecture

## AR-01 — Realm

1. **THE REALM DOMAIN** — bone info-plate: person/household at center; identity, agents, devices, compute, energy, connectivity, permissions, memory orbiting inside one boundary line.
2. **THE DOMAIN PERSISTS, THE DEVICES CHANGE** — bone info-plate: the same Realm boundary held constant across three panels while hardware inside swaps out.
3. **REALM AT THE EDGE** — riso poster: a homestead as a living edge node — antenna, solar, workshop light on, dark ridge behind.
4. **NOT A WALLET, NOT A PROFILE** — boundary card: the reductions crossed out, the domain affirmed.

## AR-02 — Firmamint

1. **SUBSTRATE, NOT SOVEREIGN** — bone info-plate: Realms, Settlemints, Districts distinct above; one thin shared substrate running beneath. Nothing sits on a throne. *(No chain imagery, no chain word.)*
2. **WHAT CROSSES THE BOUNDARY** — bone info-plate: full private records held inside each shard; only small bounded proofs crossing the membrane outward.
3. **COORDINATES · REMEMBERS · PROVES** — function triptych card, one panel per verb.

## AR-03 — The Protocol Stack

1. *(Cross-print, not new art: `operating-stack` and `operating-stack-horizontal` already illustrate the seven protocols — place them inline here.)*
2. **WHO OWNS, WHO BUILDS, WHO GOVERNS** — matrix instrument card: protocol × (owner / builder / governor), with open cells left honestly blank.
3. **THE GATE** — seal-stamped card: the five-question gate every protocol passes before its name is canon; a literal gate stamp.

## AR-04 — Governmint

1. **THE GOVERNANCE FUNCTION** — bone info-plate loop: decide → execute → review → correct, with legitimacy as the ground line.
2. **SOFTWARE ASSISTS, HUMANS GOVERN** — split plate: console below, human bench above; arrows go up as recommendations, down as decisions.

## AR-05 — Embodimint

1. **NOT A WALLET, NOT A SCORE** — bone info-plate: one person rendered as distinct layers — identity, membership, capability, contribution, reputation, authority — deliberately not merged into one number.
2. **CONTEXTUAL REPUTATION** — badge plate: the same figure trusted differently by context (builder / steward / responder), each badge scoped and dated.
3. **THE BOUNDED ATTESTATION** — bone info-plate: a thick private file inside the boundary; one small stamped card leaving it. Caption: "The proof travels. The record stays home."

## AR-06 — Basemint *(one plate only — function level)*

1. **NOT A SEALED ROOM** — bone info-plate: a settlement economy as a room with one well-made door to the outside world; value passing both ways through it. No mechanism, no rails, no currency marks. Caption: "Without trapping users inside a closed monetary environment."

## AR-07 — THEOS

1. **THE ORGANIZATIONAL NERVOUS SYSTEM** — bone info-plate: operational traces (meetings, tasks, treasury, infrastructure) flowing into one attentive layer that remembers and surfaces drift.
2. **CONSTITUTIONAL INTELLIGENCE, NOT AUTHORITY** — split plate: THEOS observes, compares, recommends, escalates; the human bench interprets, decides, amends.
3. **EVERY SETTLEMENT RUNS ITS OWN SHARD** — bone info-plate: federated shards, each with a dark private core, joined only by thin proof lines. Caption: "Federation is the anti-surveillance boundary."
4. **THE RESPONSE LEVELS** — scroll plate: Observation → Tension → Drift Warning → Boundary Breach → Escalation → Formal Exception → Constitutional Amendment.

# Volume IV — The Frontier Standard

*(Instrument register throughout: gauges, checklists, stamped cards. No scenes.)*

## ST-01 — Sovereignty and Autonomy Vectors

1. **THE TEN QUESTIONS** — instrument card: capacity through ultimate authority, as a numbered gauge panel.
2. **THE LANGUAGE LADDER** — instrument strip: Capacity → Control → Autonomy → Authority → Jurisdiction → Sovereignty, subtitled "Can do. Controls. May decide. May lawfully decide. Has the final word."
3. **THE OVERRIDE TEST** — instrument card: the seven override questions around a red override switch. Caption: "The final sovereign often appears at the point of override."

## ST-02 — Proof-of-Function Requirements

1. **THE PROOF MATRIX** — instrument card: function / frequency / reliability / independence / legitimacy / override as six dials on one panel.
2. **THE EVIDENCE-LEDGER RECORD** — specimen card: the standard record schema, filled in once, stamped.

## ST-03 — Maturity Classifications

1. **THE FOUR AXES** — compass plate: social, political, settlement, infrastructure maturity as four needles that move independently.
2. **THE STATUS SCALE** — instrument strip: proposed → prototype → pilot → operational → repeated → reliable → institutional.

## ST-04 — Governance Minimums

1. **THE MINIMUM FUNCTION SET** — checklist instrument: the functions below which "governance exists" may not be claimed; the threshold drawn as a literal line.
2. **THE LEGITIMACY REQUIREMENT** — seal card: authority without acceptance stamped INVALID; authority with acceptance sealed.

## ST-05 — External Recognition and Relationships

1. **THE NON-EQUIVALENCE RULE** — balance-scale plate: a mayor's handshake, an MOU, sovereign recognition, treaty relations — each weighing visibly differently.
2. **THE RECOGNITION REGISTER** — specimen card: how external standing is recorded — by whom, of what, with what effect, dated.

## ST-06 — Dispute and Appeals Protocols

1. **THE STANDARD QUESTIONS** — instrument card: the questions every dispute arrangement must answer.
2. **THE JURISDICTIONAL BOUNDARY** — boundary map: where internal process ends and the surrounding sovereign's law begins, drawn as a surveyed property line.
3. **THE MEMORY REQUIREMENT** — ledger plate: disputes resolved, recorded, and findable. Caption: "A resolution that cannot be remembered cannot bind."

## Workflow (same as the Volume I batch)

1. Generate from these specs (ATX Settlemint style prompts; the reference plates in `public/plates/` are the style anchors).
2. Drop finished images in `~/Downloads` or hand them to Claude.
3. Conversion: webp into `public/plates/`, kebab-case names, seals composited onto Bone.
4. Wiring: `plates:` frontmatter (src/width/height/alt/caption/display) + inline `<Plate id="…" />` in the MDX body.
5. PR → merge → Netlify deploy. Verify with a curl for `plates%2F` on each route.
